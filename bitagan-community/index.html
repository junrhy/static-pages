About Our Family

Building stronger bonds, one connection at a time

The Bitagan Family is a close-knit network of blood-related families dedicated to fostering meaningful relationships, supporting one another, and creating a nurturing environment for all members. We believe in the power of family bonds to enrich our lives and strengthen our connections.

Our family brings together relatives from all branches, united by shared values of respect, kindness, and mutual support. Through regular activities, events, and gatherings, we create opportunities for family members to connect, learn, and grow together.

🌟

Vision

The Bitagan Family envisions a legacy of generations rooted in Christ — united in faith, guided by God’s Word, and strengthened by love.

We aspire to be a family that reflects Christ in our character, decisions, and relationships. As we grow individually and together, we seek to:

  • Build a spiritually strong and prayerful family
  • Lead with integrity, humility, and compassion
  • Support and uplift one another in every season
  • Serve as a blessing to our community
  • Pass down a heritage of faith to future generations

With Christ at the center, we see a future where the Bitagan Family stands firm in purpose, walks in unity, and shines as a testimony of God’s grace and goodness.

🕊️

Mission

“As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord.”
— Joshua 24:15

The Bitagan Family is devoted to honoring God in all that we do. We place our faith at the center of our home, trusting in His guidance, wisdom, and grace.

We are committed to:

  • • Loving one another as Christ loves us. Practicing forgiveness, patience, and humility.
  • • Praying together and growing in spiritual strength.
  • • Serving others with compassion and generosity.
  • • Living with integrity as a reflection of our faith.

Through every blessing and every trial, we stand united, anchored in faith and strengthened by God’s promises.

Our mission is to build a Christ-centered home that nurtures love, inspires hope, and leaves a legacy of faith for future generations of the Bitagan Family.
