Services

Three services for clinic digital operations

Digital Operations Retainer

We manage your digital systems so your clinic runs smoothly.

Challenge

Clinics operate multiple digital systems: websites, booking platforms, patient communication tools, and internal workflows. When these systems fail, perform poorly, or don't integrate properly, operational efficiency decreases and patient care is disrupted.

Scope

  • Ongoing monitoring and maintenance of digital systems
  • Coordination between platforms and tools
  • System reliability and performance optimization
  • Technical support and troubleshooting
  • Regular system health reviews and reporting
  • Updates and improvements aligned with your clinic's operational requirements

Results

Reliable systems that function consistently month after month. Reduced operational overhead. Single point of contact for all digital operations. Predictable monthly costs instead of emergency service charges.

Ideal For

Clinics with multiple digital touchpoints requiring ongoing coordination and oversight. Appropriate when digital systems need dedicated management to maintain reliability and performance.

Monthly retainer. Pricing is determined by your clinic's system complexity and operational requirements. Consult with us to determine appropriate scope and investment.

AI Patient Inquiry Automation

Your staff stops answering repetitive questions.

Challenge

Clinic staff repeatedly answer identical patient inquiries: appointment availability, services offered, pricing, location, and hours. This work reduces time available for in-person patient care and leads to inconsistent responses or delayed replies.

Scope

  • Automated responses to common patient inquiries via website chat, email, or messaging
  • Intelligent routing of inquiries requiring human attention
  • Integration with your booking system for real-time appointment availability
  • Consistent, professional responses aligned with your clinic's communication standards
  • 24/7 availability for basic inquiries
  • Handoff protocols for complex questions or booking requests

Results

Reduced workload for front desk and administrative staff. Faster patient response times. Consistent information delivery. Staff can focus on higher-value interactions and in-person care.

Ideal For

Clinics with high volumes of patient inquiries, busy front desks, or limited administrative staff. Appropriate when you need faster response times and after-hours inquiry handling.

Monthly retainer. Initial setup includes automation configuration and staff training. Ongoing management maintains and improves system performance.

Website Growth & Conversion Care

We improve bookings, not just traffic.

Challenge

Many clinic websites receive visitors but fail to convert them into appointments. Sites may be visually adequate but don't guide visitors to book, load too slowly, or fail to communicate why patients should choose your clinic.

Scope

  • Conversion optimization focused on appointment bookings
  • Website performance improvements for faster load times
  • Strategic call-to-action placement and messaging
  • Mobile experience optimization
  • Regular testing and refinement of booking flows
  • Content improvements to communicate value clearly
  • Tracking and reporting on conversion metrics

Results

Increased appointments booked directly from your website. Improved user experience for potential patients. Better website performance. Clear metrics showing what works and what requires improvement.

Ideal For

Clinics with existing websites that need increased online bookings. Appropriate when you understand your website's importance but lack the time or expertise to optimize it continuously for conversions.

Monthly retainer. Ongoing optimization ensures continuous improvement rather than one-time fixes. Focus on measurable outcomes: increased bookings and better performance.
