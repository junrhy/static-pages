Consultation

Schedule a consultation to review your clinic's digital operations

We will review your clinic's current digital systems, operational challenges, and requirements. Based on this assessment, we will determine whether a monthly retainer partnership is appropriate for your clinic.

We will respond within 1-2 business days to schedule a consultation.

What to Expect

After You Submit

We review your information and respond within 1-2 business days via your preferred contact method. We propose a time for a call or meeting.

During the Consultation

We review your clinic, current digital systems, and operational requirements. We ask questions to understand your needs and explain how we can help. This is an assessment and information exchange.

Next Steps

If a monthly retainer partnership is appropriate, we discuss scope, investment, and expectations. If not, we provide a clear assessment and may suggest alternatives.
