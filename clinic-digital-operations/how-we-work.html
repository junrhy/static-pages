Process

Systematic approach to clinic digital operations

1

Audit & Understanding

We assess your clinic's current digital systems, workflows, and operational challenges. We review your website, booking platforms, and communication tools to identify what functions correctly and what requires improvement.

2

System Setup or Stabilization

Based on the audit, we set up new systems or stabilize existing ones. This phase focuses on reliability: ensuring systems function as expected and integrate properly. We do not add new features until the foundation is stable.

3

Automation & Optimization

Once systems are stable, we implement automation and optimization. This includes patient inquiry automation, conversion improvements, performance enhancements, and workflow optimization.

4

Ongoing Monthly Partnership

We transition to ongoing monthly retainer work: regular monitoring, maintenance, improvements, and support. Your digital operations are managed systematically so you can focus on patient care.
