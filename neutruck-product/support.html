🍎 macOS Gatekeeper Verification Guide

macOS Security Fix

When launching NeuTruck Freight for the first time on macOS, you may encounter an alert stating: "NeuTruck Freight cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified". Follow Method 1 (System Settings) or Method 2 (Right-Click) to open the app safely.

🔒 Why does macOS show this warning?

macOS Gatekeeper protects users by default when launching applications downloaded directly from the web that are not yet notarized via the Mac App Store. NeuTruck Freight is 100% safe to run.

Method 1: Override via System Settings (Recommended)

  1. 1

    Try Opening NeuTruck Freight

    Double-click NeuTruck Freight.app (or mount the downloaded .dmg file and drag it into your Applications folder). Click Done or Cancel when the prompt appears.

  2. 2

    Open System Settings

    Open System Settings (or System Preferences on older macOS versions) from your Apple Menu () at the top left of your screen.

  3. 3

    Navigate to Privacy & Security

    Click Privacy & Security on the left sidebar and scroll down to the Security section.

  4. 4

    Click "Open Anyway"

    You will see a notification stating: "NeuTruck Freight was blocked from use because it is not from an identified developer." Click the Open Anyway button next to it.

  5. 5

    Confirm Launch

    Enter your Mac administrator password or use Touch ID, then click Open in the final dialog to launch NeuTruck Freight.

Method 2: Quick Right-Click Shortcut

  1. 1

    Locate NeuTruck Freight in Finder

    Open Finder and navigate to your Applications folder where NeuTruck Freight is saved.

  2. 2

    Right-Click and Select Open

    Press and hold the Control (⌃) key and click the NeuTruck Freight icon (or right-click with your mouse/trackpad), then select Open from the context menu.

  3. 3

    Confirm Open

    Click Open in the prompt window. NeuTruck Freight will save an exception for your user account and launch normally every time hereafter.

Method 3: Terminal Command (For IT Administrators)

If you are configuring multiple dispatch terminals, you can instantly strip the quarantine attribute using Terminal:

xattr -cr "/Applications/NeuTruck Freight.app"
