🍎 macOS Gatekeeper Verification Guide

macOS Security Fix

When launching NeuGate for the first time on macOS, you may encounter an alert stating: "NeuGate cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified". Follow Method 1 (System Settings) or Method 2 (Right-Click) to open the app safely.

🔒 Why does macOS show this warning?

macOS Gatekeeper protects users by default when launching applications downloaded directly from the web that are not yet notarized via the Mac App Store. NeuGate is 100% safe to run.

Method 1: Override via System Settings (Recommended)

  1. 1

    Try Opening the NeuGate App

    Double-click NeuGate.app (or mount the downloaded .dmg file and drag NeuGate into your Applications folder). Click Done or Cancel when the prompt appears.

  2. 2

    Open System Settings

    Open System Settings (or System Preferences on older macOS versions) from your Apple Menu () at the top left of your screen.

  3. 3

    Navigate to Privacy & Security

    Click Privacy & Security on the left sidebar and scroll down to the Security section.

  4. 4

    Click "Open Anyway"

    You will see a notification stating: "NeuGate was blocked from use because it is not from an identified developer." Click the Open Anyway button next to it.

  5. 5

    Confirm Launch

    Enter your Mac administrator password or use Touch ID, then click Open in the final dialog to launch NeuGate.

Method 2: Quick Right-Click Shortcut

  1. 1

    Locate NeuGate in Finder

    Open Finder and navigate to your Applications folder where NeuGate is saved.

  2. 2

    Right-Click and Select Open

    Press and hold the Control (⌃) key and click the NeuGate icon (or right-click with your mouse/trackpad), then select Open from the context menu.

  3. 3

    Confirm Open

    Click Open in the prompt window. NeuGate will now save an exception for your user account and launch normally every time hereafter.

Method 3: Terminal Command (For IT Administrators)

If you are configuring multiple gate workstations, you can instantly strip the quarantine attribute using Terminal:

xattr -cr /Applications/NeuGate.app

🎛️ RFID Reader & Gate Setup

Hardware Setup
  1. 1

    Connect USB RFID Reader

    Plug any standard 125kHz or 13.56MHz USB RFID card reader into your station computer. NeuGate automatically listens to standard keyboard HID input events.

  2. 2

    Register Student RFID Cards

    Open NeuGate, navigate to the Students panel, and scan a student card or manually enter their student ID and parent contact number.

  3. 3

    Instant Gate Check-In & SMS Dispatch

    When students tap their card at the gate, NeuGate displays their photo badge instantly and triggers automated SMS notifications directly to parents' phones.
