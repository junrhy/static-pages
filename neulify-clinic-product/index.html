Activation

How to Get Your License Key

Neulify Clinic requires a node-locked activation license key generated specifically for your device fingerprint.

1

Get Your Machine Fingerprint

Launch the Neulify Clinic desktop app on your target computer. Copy the unique 36-character **Machine Fingerprint / UUID** displayed on the activation screen.

2

Send to Your Administrator

Send an email to hello@neulify.com (or contact your clinic IT admin) providing the following details:

  • • Client Name: Your Clinic's registered name
  • • App Name: Neulify Clinic
  • • Machine ID: Your copied Machine Fingerprint / UUID
3

Activate Your Desktop App

The administrator will generate your customized activation key (e.g. NEUL-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Paste this key back into the app activation window to unlock Neulify Clinic offline.
