Complete Barangay LGU Digital Platform

Streamlined resident records, barangay services, RFID timekeeping, & honorarium payroll solution.

👥

Resident Profiling & Directory

Maintain detailed household and resident information, voter status, sector classification (PWD, Senior Citizen, Solo Parent), and digital IDs.

📜

Certificates & Clearances

Instant generation and printing of Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Indigency, Certificate of Residency, and Business Permits with digital verification stamps.

⚖️

Blotter & Incident Records

Record, log, and track barangay peace and order complaints, hearing schedules, settlements, and Lupon Tagapamayapa case files.

🪪

RFID Staff & Official Access

Track attendance of Barangay Officials, Tanods, Health Workers (BHWs), and Staff using RFID cards, biometric scanners, or QR passes.

💰

Honorarium & Staff Payroll

Automate monthly honoraria and staff payroll computations with late minute deductions, leave calculations, and printable payroll sheets.

📑

Official Form 48 DTR Exporter

Export and print Civil Service Form No. 48 Daily Time Records complete with official 31-day AM/PM logs and Captain/Administrator sign-offs.

💬

Automated SMS Announcements

Dispatch SMS announcements, barangay advisories, and clearance readiness alerts directly to residents and officials via SMS gateway integration.

✍️

Manual Attendance Override

Administrator manual attendance override console for field duties, official business (OB) logs, and biometric fallbacks.

🛡️

Role & Security Controls

Granular permission control (Barangay Captain, Secretary, Treasurer, Tanod) to protect sensitive resident records and financial ledgers.
