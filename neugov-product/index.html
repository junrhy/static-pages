Complete Barangay Information Management System (BIMS)

Powerful desktop LGU platform combining RFID access, resident & official directory, leave & OT approvals, honorarium payroll, and SMS notifications.

🚪

Barangay Hall Gate Console

Real-time RFID, biometric, & barcode scan console with audio chimes, scan cooldown protection, custom background wallpapers, and live gate status feeds.

👥

Resident & Official Directory

Comprehensive profiling for Barangay Officials, Tanods, BHWs, and Residents. Manage digital IDs, emergency contacts, sector tags (PWD, Senior, Solo Parent), and photo attachments.

⏰

Work Schedules & Duty Shifts

Configure custom work schedules, daily shift hours, grace periods, and break rules tailored for barangay hall personnel, night-shift tanods, and health workers.

📜

Attendance Logs & Manual Override

Full 31-day attendance log inspection with filterable date ranges, status tracking (On Time, Late, Undertime, Absent), and administrator manual entry overrides for field duties.

📋

Civil Service Form No. 48 DTR Exporter

Generate and print official Philippine Daily Time Records (Form 48) complete with 31-day AM/PM in/out stamps and Barangay Captain verification signatures.

🌴

Leave & Absence Management

File and process Sick Leave (SL), Vacation Leave (VL), Emergency Leave, and Official Business (OB) field trip requests with manager approval workflows.

⏱️

Overtime & Disaster Duty Approvals

Pre-authorize extended barangay outpost or emergency response overtime shifts with manager sign-offs that flow straight into payroll computations.

💰

Barangay Honorarium & Payroll Engine

Automate monthly honoraria and staff payroll cut-offs with custom basic rates, overtime pay, late minute deductions, and printable payslip slips.

🎁

13th Month & Bonus Processor

Calculate end-of-year 13th-month bonus payouts compliant with Philippine labor laws and BIR TRAIN tax exemption thresholds.

📊

LGU HR Analytics & Reports

Visual analytics dashboards providing real-time insights into staff attendance rates, punctuality trends, monthly payroll expenses, and department metrics.

💬

SMS Broadcast & Alert Gateway

Dispatch real-time resident check-in SMS notifications, emergency advisories, and official alerts via Pusher, Semaphore, UniSMS, PhilSMS, or local Android gateway.

🛡️

Role Access & Fullscreen Kiosk Mode

Role-based security permissions (Admin, Barangay Captain, HR Staff) with one-click kiosk mode to lock the gate console on turnstile stations.
